## 1. Plain Markdown

A relative path is optimised at build time and gets the prose border/radius:

![A soft gradient](./sample.jpg)

## 2. `<Figure>` with a caption

`.mdx` posts can use components. `<Figure>` wraps an optimised image and an
optional caption:

<Figure src={sample} alt="A soft gradient" caption="Figure 1: an imported image, rendered through <Figure>." />

Pass `wide` to let it spill past the reading column:

<Figure src={sample} alt="A soft gradient" caption="The same image, wide." wide />

## 3. Raw `<Image>`
