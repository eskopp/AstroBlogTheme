The first two minutes of a launch are where almost everything happens. The
vehicle burns through most of its propellant, passes through the region of
maximum aerodynamic pressure, and still has to hit its window to the second.

If you archive the stream, you can go back afterwards and watch the ascent frame
by frame long after the live broadcast has ended. That is the whole point of
keeping a copy.
